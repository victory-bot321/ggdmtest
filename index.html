Meet the G.O

Prophet Ifeanyi Israel — General Overseer

Prophet Ifeanyi Israel is a seasoned man of God with a mandate to bring deliverance, healing, and prophetic direction to the nations. With a deep well of spiritual insight, he has seen countless lives transformed through the power of the Holy Spirit.

My heart is to see every believer walk in their God‑given destiny — free from bondage, full of faith, and burning with passion for Christ.

Under his leadership, Gateway Global Deliverance Ministry has grown into a beacon of hope, drawing people from all walks of life into a deeper encounter with God.
